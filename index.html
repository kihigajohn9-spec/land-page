Questions

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in Dar es Salaam within 48 hours. Arusha, Dodoma and Mwanza deliveries take 3 to 4 days.

Can I skip or cancel?

Yes. Skip a delivery or cancel up to 24 hours before roasting, with no fee.

Which grind should I choose?

Choose whole bean if you have a grinder. Otherwise tell us your brewer and we grind to match.

How do I pay?

M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money or card. You are charged when each bag ships.
